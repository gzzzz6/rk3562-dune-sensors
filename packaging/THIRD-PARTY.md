# Bundled software

- Neptus: [LSTS/neptus at 9fe8db6](https://github.com/LSTS/neptus/tree/9fe8db6d61ced2f9163023fbedbf689e8eaa55c9). The sensor bench bundle selects the IMC monitor plugin and includes the upstream license and notices in `app/LICENSE.md` and `app/legal`. Libraries keep their embedded notices. This is a sensor bench distribution, with a separate console and UDP preference.
- Java: Eclipse Temurin 11, linked from the Windows x64 Temurin 11.0.32.1+1 JDK. Its notices are in `java/legal`; the [corresponding OpenJDK source](https://github.com/adoptium/jdk11u/tree/c663d6d002c4ee86b4c3b53788d5be8955b15b0b) and [Temurin build scripts](https://github.com/adoptium/temurin-build/tree/e6ba7dec3d07654074559310376a3ae89da5f4ac) are available upstream.
